📖 How to Play

Super easy and fun for everyone!

🎯

1. Pull & Aim the Slingshot

Drag the snack backward with your mouse or finger. Follow the white dotted trajectory line to aim!

🎶

2. Bounce off Musical Chimes

Singing crystal bells make real harmonious music when hit and give big combo bonus points!

🌈

3. Magic Color Mixers

Shoot through colored portals to blend snacks: Yellow + Blue = Green Melon! Red + Yellow = Orange Tangerine!

🥰

4. Feed Hungry Critters

Look at their thought bubble to see what snack they crave. Feed them to make them smile and dance!
